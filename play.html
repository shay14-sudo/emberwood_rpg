The Rusty Kettle Tavern

The innkeeper — an old man with a scar-notched ear — slides a mug your way before you've even sat down.

"New face. Here for the notice board, I'd wager." He nods toward the door. "Ember's been restless. Wolves gone half-mad on the north road, goblin raiders pushing in from the hollow, and — gods help us — the old battlefield past the ridge is giving up its dead again. Skeletons, if you can believe it."

He leans in. "Nobody knows what's waking it after all these centuries. Could be nothing. Could be something worse than nothing. Either way, Ashmere's guard is stretched thin, and the town's offering what coin and gear it can spare to anyone willing to look into it."

He straightens up, wiping the counter. "So. You in — or are you just here for the ale?"
