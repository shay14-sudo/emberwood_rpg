The World

Generations ago, a fragment of a fallen star buried itself in the roots of the forest west of Ashmere, and never went cold. The town learned to live beside it — the trees that glow faintly at dusk, the springs that taste faintly of copper, the deer that never seem to age. For a long time, it slept.

It doesn't anymore.

What started on the north road hasn't stayed there. Past the ridge, an old battlefield is giving up its dead. Deeper still, a sunken bog has started producing things that shouldn't be able to walk, and an abandoned mine has gone from silent to occupied. Furthest out, the Blightwood — once just an unpleasant stretch of trees — is spreading, slow and sure, toward Ashmere's fields. Each place answers to something guarding it, older and worse than what wanders its edges.

Nobody in Ashmere claims to understand what's waking. Captain Rowan keeps a running account of it on the town's Notice Board — one job at a time, one answer at a time — because that's the only way anyone's found to make the problem small enough to actually start on.

"Ashmere's guard is stretched thin, and the town's offering what coin and gear it can spare to anyone willing to look into it." — Captain Rowan

What Awaits

Emberwood isn't just fights strung together. Wander the forest and you'll find quiet roads as often as monsters — a traveler who needs help, a chest half-buried in leaf litter, a path that's simply empty. Ashmere's Notice Board keeps a running account of the town's troubles, from thinning the wolves to whatever's rising past the ridge. Gear up at the Market with loot you've earned, spend skill points where your build wants them, and grow — level by level — into whoever you're going to become out there.

The forest is waiting.
