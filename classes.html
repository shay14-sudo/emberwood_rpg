The Three Paths

Choose who you are before you find out what Emberwood is.

Fighter
Steel and grit. You go where the fighting is worst — and make sure you're the one still standing.

Wade in with a blade in one hand and a shield in the other, or give up the shield for a greatsword that hits twice as hard. Break enemy lines with Bash, and grow into a master of Sword and Shield or the Great Weapon.

STR drives your Attack directly. CON keeps you standing with more Max HP, and a little DEX helps you hit and dodge more often.

Active

  • Bash — reliable power hit
  • Shield Slam — 1H + shield only
  • Cleaving Strike — 2H weapon only

Passive

  • Sword & Shield Mastery
  • Great Weapon Mastery
  • Toughness
Archer
You've hunted deeper into Emberwood than most dare. You know its paths, and its silence, better than anyone.

Loose two arrows at once with Double Shot, choose between the reach of a longbow or the punch of a crossbow, and let Sharp Eye turn a clean hit into a critical one.

DEX sharpens both your accuracy and your Defense. STR still fuels your Attack behind the bow, and LUK boosts how often you land a Critical Hit.

Active

  • Double Shot — two arrows, one turn
  • Piercing Shot — bow only, can't miss
  • Rapid Reload — crossbow only, huge crit

Passive

  • Longbow Mastery
  • Crossbow Mastery
  • Sharp Eye
Mage
You've felt the Ember's pull for years — a hum at the edge of your dreams. Now you mean to answer it.

Choose the spells that define you: elemental fire and frost, psychic tricks that stun and drain, or wards that turn incoming pain into mana. No two Mages build the same way twice.

INT fuels both your Magic Attack and your Mana pool. CON keeps you alive at range, and a little DEX helps your spells land.

Elemental

  • Fireball
  • Frost Lance — chills the target
  • Lightning Bolt — high crit

Psychic

  • Mind Spike — chance to stun
  • Psychic Drain — damage + heal

Buff

  • Mana Shield
  • Arcane Ward
  • Quickened Mind
